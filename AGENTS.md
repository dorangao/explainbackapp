# ExplainBack contributor instructions

- Keep `docs/architecture.md` and its technology stack current in the same change whenever architecture, integrations, model roles, storage, or deployment workflows change. Verify the document against the implementation; do not record secrets or private invite links.
- Record shared implementation, verification, commit, push, and deployment states separately in the shared ExplainBack progress ledger when working across the user's ExplainBack checkouts.
- Preserve the human review boundary: generated lessons, formulas, claims, and model feedback are not automatically verified science or evidence of teaching effectiveness.
